# Pricello Privacy Policy

<div class="language-switcher">
  <a href="#english">English</a> · <a href="#espanol">Español</a>
</div>


**Effective date:** September 30, 2026  
**Contact:** jghulami2000@gmail.com

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Pricello ("Pricello," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information when you use the Pricello mobile application, related services, and support channels.

Pricello is a grocery-shopping assistant. It may help users create and reuse shopping lists, prepare for shopping, compare eligible price information, view estimates, record shopping activity, discover stores, and use optional receipt-related features.

This policy applies to personal information processed through Pricello. It does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services that you choose to use or that Pricello lawfully relies on.

## 2. Information We May Process

The information processed depends on which features you use.

### Account and profile information

If you create an account, Pricello may process:

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- ZIP code
- Optional street address
- Authentication and account identifiers
- Shopping-area and store preferences
- Language and appearance preferences

Passwords are handled through the authentication system and are not intended to be stored by Pricello in readable plain text.

### Shopping information

Pricello may process information you create or save, including:

- Shopping lists
- Product names
- Quantities
- Product sizes
- Brand preferences
- Organic or regular preferences
- Preferred stores
- Store memberships
- Draft and ready-to-shop lists
- Shopping sessions
- Purchased or skipped item status
- Estimated prices
- User-entered actual prices
- Favorites and recent items
- Shopping history

### Location and shopping-area information

Pricello uses a ZIP code or shopping area to provide geographically relevant functionality.

A street address is optional unless a specific optional feature clearly requires it.

If you choose to grant device-location permission, Pricello may process location information to help identify nearby stores or improve local results. Pricello is not intended to require continuous background location tracking for normal shopping-list use.

If precise location is not granted, supported features should continue to use ZIP code, manually selected shopping area, or other less precise location information where practical.

### Receipt information

If receipt features are enabled and you choose to use them, Pricello may process:

- Receipt images
- Store name and location
- Receipt date
- Purchased products
- Quantities
- Prices
- Currency
- Totals
- Product identifiers
- Other text appearing on the receipt

Receipts may contain information that Pricello does not need, such as loyalty identifiers, partial payment-card details, or other personal information. Do not intentionally upload sensitive information that is unnecessary for the shopping feature.

Camera access should be requested only when you choose a feature that requires it.

### Store and community-contributed information

Pricello may allow users to help identify stores that are missing from the store directory.

Information used for that purpose may include:

- Store name
- Store address
- ZIP or postal code
- Store or branch number, when explicitly available
- Country
- General store location
- Evidence that helps distinguish one branch from another

A store detected from one user's receipt should not automatically become a verified public store. Pricello may compare the information with existing records or other permitted sources before making it broadly discoverable.

Pricello does not intend to publicly identify the user who contributed a store merely because non-personal store information becomes part of the store directory.

### Technical and diagnostic information

Pricello may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as:

- App version
- Device or operating-system information
- Error and diagnostic events
- Request status and timing
- Security-related events

Pricello is designed to avoid placing passwords, authentication tokens, full street addresses, raw grocery lists, raw receipt contents, or payment information into ordinary analytics events.

## 3. Sources of Information

Pricello may receive information:

- Directly from you
- From your device when you grant an optional permission
- From your prior activity within Pricello
- From permitted open-data or third-party data providers
- From store, product, geographic, or price sources that Pricello is authorized to use

Examples of non-user data sources may include USDA FoodData Central, Open Prices/Open Food Facts, OpenStreetMap, and other providers that may be added later.

Public availability of data does not automatically mean Pricello treats that data as authorized for every use. Pricello uses source, provenance, freshness, and eligibility controls to determine whether information may be used in particular features.

## 4. How We Use Information

Pricello may use personal information to:

- Create and maintain accounts
- Authenticate users
- Maintain guest or signed-in shopping lists
- Save shopping preferences
- Provide shopping checklists
- Show relevant stores
- Estimate grocery costs
- Compare eligible shopping options
- Maintain shopping history
- Process optional receipt features
- Match receipts to products or stores
- Detect duplicate or inaccurate store records
- Improve reliability and usability
- Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
- Diagnose technical problems
- Respond to support and privacy requests
- Comply with applicable law

We do not use private grocery-list contents to secretly alter rankings for paid placement.

## 5. Price Information

Pricello may display observed prices, general estimates, optimized basket estimates, user-confirmed prices, or other price information obtained from eligible sources.

Price information may be historical, incomplete, estimated, or unavailable.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Pricello does not represent a displayed price as a guaranteed, live, or current retailer price.

## 6. Service Providers and Disclosures

Pricello may disclose information to service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, such as providers of:

- Authentication
- Database or cloud hosting
- Error monitoring
- Address search
- Store or location search
- Receipt processing
- Product information
- Price information

Pricello currently uses Supabase-related services for portions of authentication and application infrastructure.

Additional providers may be introduced as the product develops. Before a provider is used to process materially new categories of personal information, Pricello should update its disclosures as required by applicable law and platform policy.

Pricello may also disclose information:

- At your direction
- To protect users, the service, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats
- To comply with valid legal process or applicable law
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable legal requirements

## 7. No Sale of Personal Information; No Targeted Advertising

Pricello does not currently sell personal information for monetary consideration.

Pricello does not currently use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising based on activity across unrelated services.

If these practices materially change, Pricello will update this Privacy Policy and provide any legally required notice, consent, or opt-out mechanism before implementing the change.

## 8. Sensitive Information

Pricello is designed to minimize the collection of sensitive personal information.

Precise geolocation, if enabled, should be used only for a feature you request and not for unrelated purposes.

Pricello is not designed to collect full payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, health diagnoses, biometric identifiers, or other highly sensitive information as part of ordinary grocery-shopping functionality.

Do not intentionally submit such information unless a future feature expressly requires it and provides an appropriate disclosure.

## 9. Data Retention

Pricello retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Maintain account and shopping functionality
- Protect security and prevent fraud
- Resolve disputes
- Meet legitimate recordkeeping needs
- Comply with applicable law

Retention may differ by data type and feature.

When an account is deleted, account-linked personal information will be deleted or de-identified as required by applicable law and the applicable deletion process, except for information that Pricello is legally required or permitted to retain.

Irreversibly de-identified or aggregated information may be retained.

Verified non-personal store facts may remain after the connection to the contributing user has been removed.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

Registered users can initiate account deletion from the in-app account settings.

Users who cannot access the app may request deletion by emailing:

**jghulami2000@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**Pricello Account Deletion Request**

Do not send your password, authentication token, or payment information.

Pricello may take reasonable steps to verify that the requester is authorized to delete the account.

Additional deletion information is provided in the Pricello Account & Data Deletion page.

## 11. Guest Data

Guest Mode may store information locally on the device and, depending on the feature, may also interact with Pricello services.

Pricello provides controls for deleting applicable guest data.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored guest information, but it may not by itself delete information previously transmitted to a service. Use available deletion controls or contact Pricello when you want server-side personal information deleted.

## 12. Privacy Rights in the United States

Residents of certain U.S. states may have privacy rights under applicable law. Depending on the law and whether it applies to Pricello, those rights may include the right to:

- Confirm whether personal data is being processed
- Access personal data
- Correct inaccuracies
- Delete personal data
- Obtain a portable copy of certain personal data
- Opt out of certain sales, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling
- Appeal certain decisions concerning a privacy request
- Exercise rights without unlawful discrimination

Pricello does not currently sell personal information or process personal information for targeted advertising.

To submit a privacy request, email:

**jghulami2000@gmail.com**

Use a clear subject such as:

- Pricello Privacy Request
- Pricello Data Access Request
- Pricello Data Correction Request
- Pricello Data Deletion Request

Pricello may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Where applicable law provides a right to appeal a denied privacy request, reply to the decision email with the subject:

**Pricello Privacy Appeal**

Pricello will handle validated requests within the time required by applicable law.

If a request is denied, Pricello will provide any explanation or appeal information required by applicable law.

## 13. Texas Residents

To the extent the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act applies to Pricello and the relevant processing, Texas residents may have rights to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of personal data, and to opt out of certain sales, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling.

Pricello does not currently sell personal information or use personal information for targeted advertising.

Requests and appeals may be submitted through the contact methods described above.

Nothing in this section is intended to limit rights available under applicable Texas law.

## 14. California and Other State Privacy Laws

Residents of California and other states may have additional rights if the relevant law applies to Pricello.

Pricello will honor legally applicable rights and disclosures, including applicable rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, sale/sharing, targeted advertising, sensitive personal information, and non-discrimination.

Because legal thresholds and definitions vary by state and may change over time, this Policy does not state that every state privacy law applies to Pricello in every circumstance.

## 15. Children

Pricello is a general-audience grocery-shopping service and is not directed to children under 13.

Pricello does not knowingly seek to collect personal information online from children under 13.

If you are under 13, do not create an account or submit personal information through Pricello.

If Pricello learns that personal information was collected from a child under 13 in circumstances covered by applicable law, Pricello will take appropriate steps to address or delete the information.

Parents or guardians may contact:

**jghulami2000@gmail.com**

## 16. Security

Pricello uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information.

These may include:

- Encrypted network communications
- Authentication controls
- Authorization checks
- Access restrictions
- Input validation
- Separation of development and production environments
- Security testing
- Dependency review
- Logging designed to avoid sensitive content

No system can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your Pricello account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly.

## 17. International Use and Cross-Border Processing

Pricello is currently designed primarily for users in the United States, but some features may process information relating to stores or receipts outside the United States.

Service providers may process information in jurisdictions different from where you live.

Prices from different countries or currencies should not automatically be combined in U.S. price comparisons.

## 18. Third-Party Services and Links

Pricello may link to or rely on independent third-party services.

Those third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Pricello is not responsible for independent third-party practices outside Pricello's control, but Pricello will seek to use providers appropriate for the relevant feature and applicable legal requirements.

## 19. Changes to This Policy

Pricello may update this Privacy Policy when:

- Features change
- Data practices change
- Service providers change
- Legal requirements change
- Platform requirements change

The updated version will display a new effective date.

Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent for a material change, Pricello will provide it before the new practice takes effect.

## 20. Contact

# Política de Privacidad de Pricello

**Fecha de entrada en vigor:** 30 de septiembre de 2026

> Esta versión en español pretende comunicar sustancialmente el mismo contenido que la versión en inglés. Si existiera una diferencia material entre ambas versiones, se aplicará la versión en inglés en la medida permitida por la ley aplicable.

## 1. Alcance

Esta Política de Privacidad explica cómo Pricello ("Pricello", "nosotros", "nos" o "nuestro") trata la información personal cuando usted utiliza la aplicación móvil Pricello, los servicios relacionados y los canales de soporte.

Pricello es un asistente para compras de comestibles. Puede ayudar a los usuarios a crear y reutilizar listas de compras, prepararse para comprar, comparar información de precios elegible, ver estimaciones, registrar actividad de compras, descubrir tiendas y utilizar funciones opcionales relacionadas con recibos.

Esta política se aplica a la información personal tratada a través de Pricello. No controla las prácticas de privacidad independientes de servicios de terceros que usted decida utilizar o en los que Pricello se apoye legítimamente.

## 2. Información que podemos tratar

La información tratada depende de las funciones que usted utilice.

### Información de cuenta y perfil

Si crea una cuenta, Pricello puede tratar:

- Nombre
- Apellido
- Dirección de correo electrónico
- Código postal
- Dirección física opcional
- Identificadores de autenticación y de cuenta
- Preferencias de zona de compra y tiendas
- Preferencias de idioma y apariencia

Las contraseñas se gestionan mediante el sistema de autenticación y no están destinadas a almacenarse por Pricello en texto plano legible.

### Información de compras

Pricello puede tratar información que usted cree o guarde, incluyendo:

- Listas de compras
- Nombres de productos
- Cantidades
- Tamaños de productos
- Preferencias de marca
- Preferencias entre productos orgánicos o regulares
- Tiendas preferidas
- Membresías de tiendas
- Listas en borrador y listas listas para comprar
- Sesiones de compra
- Estado de artículos comprados u omitidos
- Precios estimados
- Precios reales introducidos por el usuario
- Favoritos y artículos recientes
- Historial de compras

### Información de ubicación y zona de compra

Pricello utiliza un código postal o una zona de compra para proporcionar funciones geográficamente relevantes.

La dirección física es opcional, salvo que una función opcional específica la requiera claramente.

Si decide conceder permiso de ubicación al dispositivo, Pricello puede tratar información de ubicación para ayudar a identificar tiendas cercanas o mejorar resultados locales. Pricello no está diseñado para requerir seguimiento continuo de ubicación en segundo plano para el uso normal de listas de compras.

Si no concede ubicación precisa, las funciones compatibles deberían seguir utilizando el código postal, una zona de compra seleccionada manualmente u otra información de ubicación menos precisa cuando sea razonablemente posible.

### Información de recibos

Si las funciones de recibos están habilitadas y usted decide utilizarlas, Pricello puede tratar:

- Imágenes de recibos
- Nombre y ubicación de la tienda
- Fecha del recibo
- Productos comprados
- Cantidades
- Precios
- Moneda
- Totales
- Identificadores de productos
- Otro texto que aparezca en el recibo

Los recibos pueden contener información que Pricello no necesita, como identificadores de programas de fidelidad, dígitos parciales de tarjetas de pago u otra información personal. No cargue intencionalmente información sensible que no sea necesaria para la función de compras.

El acceso a la cámara solo debería solicitarse cuando usted elija una función que lo requiera.

### Información de tiendas y contribuciones de la comunidad

Pricello puede permitir que los usuarios ayuden a identificar tiendas que aún no estén en el directorio de tiendas.

La información utilizada para ese fin puede incluir:

- Nombre de la tienda
- Dirección de la tienda
- Código postal
- Número de tienda o sucursal, cuando esté disponible de forma explícita
- País
- Ubicación general de la tienda
- Evidencia que ayude a distinguir una sucursal de otra

Una tienda detectada a partir del recibo de un solo usuario no debería convertirse automáticamente en una tienda pública verificada. Pricello puede comparar la información con registros existentes u otras fuentes permitidas antes de hacerla ampliamente visible.

Pricello no pretende identificar públicamente al usuario que contribuyó una tienda únicamente porque información no personal de esa tienda pase a formar parte del directorio.

### Información técnica y de diagnóstico

Pricello puede tratar información técnica limitada necesaria para operar, proteger y solucionar problemas del servicio, como:

- Versión de la aplicación
- Información del dispositivo o sistema operativo
- Eventos de error y diagnóstico
- Estado y tiempos de solicitudes
- Eventos relacionados con seguridad

Pricello está diseñado para evitar incluir contraseñas, tokens de autenticación, direcciones físicas completas, listas de compras sin procesar, contenido completo de recibos o información de pago en eventos ordinarios de analítica.

## 3. Fuentes de la información

Pricello puede recibir información:

- Directamente de usted
- De su dispositivo cuando concede un permiso opcional
- De su actividad previa dentro de Pricello
- De proveedores permitidos de datos abiertos o de terceros
- De fuentes de tiendas, productos, geografía o precios que Pricello esté autorizado a utilizar

Ejemplos de fuentes de datos no proporcionados por usuarios pueden incluir USDA FoodData Central, Open Prices/Open Food Facts, OpenStreetMap y otros proveedores que puedan añadirse posteriormente.

La disponibilidad pública de datos no significa automáticamente que Pricello considere esos datos autorizados para cualquier uso. Pricello utiliza controles de fuente, procedencia, antigüedad y elegibilidad para determinar si la información puede utilizarse en determinadas funciones.

## 4. Cómo utilizamos la información

Pricello puede utilizar información personal para:

- Crear y mantener cuentas
- Autenticar usuarios
- Mantener listas de compras de invitados o usuarios registrados
- Guardar preferencias de compra
- Proporcionar listas de verificación para compras

- Diagnosticar problemas técnicos
- Responder solicitudes de soporte y privacidad
- Cumplir la ley aplicable

No utilizamos el contenido privado de las listas de compras para alterar secretamente clasificaciones a favor de ubicaciones pagadas.

## 5. Información de precios

Pricello puede mostrar precios observados, estimaciones generales, estimaciones de canastas optimizadas, precios confirmados por usuarios u otra información de precios obtenida de fuentes elegibles.

La información de precios puede ser histórica, incompleta, estimada o no estar disponible.

Salvo indicación expresa en contrario, Pricello no representa un precio mostrado como un precio garantizado, en vivo o necesariamente vigente del minorista.

## 6. Proveedores de servicios y divulgaciones

Pricello puede divulgar información a proveedores de servicios únicamente cuando sea razonablemente necesario para operar el servicio, incluidos proveedores de:

- Autenticación

- Para proteger a usuarios, al servicio o a terceros frente a fraude, abuso o amenazas de seguridad
